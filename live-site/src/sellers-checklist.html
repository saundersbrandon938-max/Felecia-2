The Columbus Home Seller’s Checklist

Every step from your home value estimate to closing day, with notes for PCS moves and estate sales.

Felecia Saunders

Associate Broker/Owner
NextHome Infinite Real Estate Solutions
Licensed in Georgia and Alabama

1

Plan your sale Know your numbers first

  • Decide when you need to move and where you’re going next
  • Ask your lender for your mortgage payoff amount
  • Get a free home value estimate from Felecia, based on recent sales near you
  • Estimate what you’ll take home after the payoff, commission, closing costs and repairs
  • Gather paperwork: your closing papers from when you bought, survey, HOA details, warranties and recent utility bills
  • Selling on PCS orders: share your report date so we can plan backward from it
2

Get the home ready First impressions sell homes

  • Declutter and pack what you won’t need for the next few months
  • Deep clean, including windows, carpets and baseboards
  • Fix the small things: leaky faucets, loose handles, burned-out bulbs, paint touch-ups
  • Boost curb appeal: mow, trim, edge, and refresh the front door
  • Ask Felecia which repairs are worth doing and which to skip
  • Collect receipts for upgrades like a new roof or heating and air system
3

List and show Get it in front of the right buyers

  • Set your list price with Felecia using recent nearby sales
  • Schedule professional photos
  • Agree on how showings will work and how much notice you need
  • Lock away valuables, medications and personal papers before showings
  • Keep the home show-ready and plan where pets will go during showings
4

Offers and under contract Compare more than the price

  • Compare offers on price, financing, closing date and contingencies
  • Fill out the seller’s disclosure honestly. Felecia will explain what’s expected in Georgia or Alabama
  • Respond to the buyer’s inspection and repair requests
  • Know that VA and FHA appraisals can require certain repairs before closing
  • Keep the lawn, utilities and insurance up until closing

5

Closing day Hand over the keys

  • Schedule utilities to transfer or shut off the day after closing
  • Plan your move-out so the home is empty and clean for the final walk-through
  • Leave keys, garage remotes, manuals and warranty papers
  • Wire fraud alert: confirm any payment instructions by calling your closing office at a number you trust
  • Bring your photo ID to closing
  • Forward your mail, and cancel homeowners insurance only after closing

Questions to ask before you list

  • What have similar homes near me sold for lately?
  • Which repairs give the best return, and which can I skip?
  • How long are homes like mine taking to sell?
  • About how much will I take home after all costs?
  • When is a good time to list in Columbus?
  • How will you market my home?

Selling an inherited or estate home

Handling a loved one’s home from another state? Felecia can coordinate repairs, access and paperwork while you’re away. Ask an estate attorney who has the authority to sell.

Selling in Georgia or Alabama

Disclosure rules and closing customs differ between the two states. Felecia is licensed in both and will walk you through what applies to your home.
