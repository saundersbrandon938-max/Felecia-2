My story

Hello, my name is Felecia Saunders, and I am a licensed Real Estate Broker serving clients throughout Georgia and Alabama. My approach to real estate is built on the core principles of communication, dedication, commitment, and trust. I believe every real estate transaction should be centered around the client’s goals and satisfaction, whether they are buying, selling, or investing in property.

With 28 years of experience working for a Fortune 500 company and 20 years as a real estate professional, I bring a strong foundation of professionalism, customer service, and problem-solving to every transaction. My mission is to educate, guide, and advocate for my clients while providing exceptional service throughout the entire real estate process.

My family’s deep military roots have given me the privilege of connecting with and serving people from diverse backgrounds and walks of life. This experience has strengthened my understanding of the unique needs of military families, relocations, and life transitions.

Being entrusted as your Realtor is a responsibility I do not take lightly. I am committed to earning your trust, protecting your interests, and helping you achieve your real estate goals with confidence. Whether you are purchasing your first home, selling a property, expanding your investment portfolio, or relocating, I am dedicated to providing the highest level of service and expertise.

I look forward to the opportunity to work for you and to be your trusted advocate for all your real estate needs.
